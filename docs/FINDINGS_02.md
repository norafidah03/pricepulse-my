# Findings 02 · What is driving grocery prices? (Q2)

Data: KPDN PriceCatcher via data.gov.my, comparing Aug to Oct 2026 with Aug to Oct 2025 unless stated.
Observed price changes, not official inflation. Notebook: `notebooks/03_drivers_basket.py`.

## Headlines
1. **Vegetables are the story of 2026.** Tomatoes are up 63% on a year earlier, kailan 43%, Japanese and green
   mustard greens (sawi) 31 to 34%, round brinjal 30%. Tomato is the fastest-rising item in 13 of 16 states.
2. **Beef and mutton add the most to the national rise** (+0.43 pp of the +0.8% year-on-year in Oct 2026),
   followed by vegetables (+0.37 pp), onions, chilli and spices (+0.33 pp) and chicken (+0.28 pp).
3. **Fruit, rice and fish pull prices down.** Imported rice (e.g. Faiza Emas 10 kg) is 6% cheaper; broccoli
   (-16%), cauliflower (-14%) and ginger (-12%) fell most.
4. **States differ mainly through fish.** Fish has the largest CPI food weight. It pushed Kedah to the top
   (+0.61 pp) and Sabah into negative territory (-2.27 pp).
5. **Local exceptions:** in Terengganu, imported rose shallots more than doubled (+133%); in Sabah the top risers
   are red and green bird's-eye chilli (+50% and +44%); in Labuan, Beijing cabbage (+46%).

## Notes
- National rankings only include items priced by at least 100 matched shops in each of the last 3 months.
  State rankings need at least 3 matched shops in that state in each of those months.
- Fresh produce is seasonal and volatile, which is why the dashboard compares the same 3 months a year apart.
- Item names are still PriceCatcher's Malay names; English labels come with the dashboard (M5).
